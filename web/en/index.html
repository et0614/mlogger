M-Logger, the Thermal Environment Measurement System

A compact instrument for measuring the indoor thermal environment of buildings.

  • Measures dry-bulb temperature, relative humidity, globe temperature, low air velocity, illuminance and CO2 concentration
  • Calculates thermal comfort indices (PMV, PPD, SET*) in real time
  • Runs on two AA batteries (USB power also possible)
  • Operated and collects data wirelessly from a smartphone (iPhone / Android) or a PC
  • All design data is published (open-source hardware)
